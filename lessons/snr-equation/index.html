Strategies by sequence

Abdomen T1-weighted 3D GRE

The hard limit is the breath hold. Buy time with parallel imaging and partial Fourier, then reinvest it in resolution or coverage. Exceeding about 20 seconds trades noise for breathing ghosts, which are far worse. Use the widest bandwidth that keeps chemical shift under a pixel.

Abdomen T2-weighted TSE

Echo train length sets how many breath holds you need. Phase encode anterior–posterior to use a rectangular FOV and keep aortic pulsation ghosts off the liver; add oversampling if the arms or flanks wrap.

DWI (single-shot EPI)

The weakest-SNR sequence, so it gets averages. Parallel imaging and partial Fourier shorten the echo train, which reduces distortion and TE; the shorter TE partly repays the g-factor penalty. Keep the matrix modest: phase resolution lengthens the train.

Prostate (PI-RADS v2.1)

T2W: 3 mm slices, in-plane ≤ 0.7 mm (phase) by ≤ 0.4 mm (frequency). DWI: ≤ 4 mm slices, in-plane ≤ 2.5 mm, FOV 16–22 cm. DCE: 3D T1 GRE, ≤ 2 mm in-plane, temporal resolution ≤ 15 s. Small FOVs need phase oversampling to avoid wrap from the hips and buttocks.

Measuring SNR without fooling yourself

Mean over standard deviation needs a valid noise estimate. The classic "signal ROI divided by background-air SD" assumes uniform noise. That breaks with multichannel coils, parallel imaging (noise varies with the g-factor map), partial Fourier, filtering and magnitude reconstruction (Rician noise in air). It can overstate SNR substantially.

Better methods. Subtract two identical acquisitions and measure the SD of the difference (divided by √2), or acquire a noise-only scan, or use the scanner's SNR-unit reconstruction where available. Measure in the organ you care about, because coil sensitivity makes SNR position-dependent.

Compare protocols fairly. Report voxel size, scan time and SNR together, or compare SNR efficiency (SNR per √time). A protocol that looks better may simply be longer or coarser.

SNR is modeled with the standard proportionality above, a simple g-factor table and illustrative tissue relaxation values; the baseline protocols are calibrated to plausible organ SNR, not to any scanner. Minimum TE/TR, echo spacing and EPI timing follow simplified formulas. Wrap, pulsation and breathing ghosts, chemical shift and EPI distortion are rendered geometrically for intuition, and through-plane partial volume is applied to the lesion only.
